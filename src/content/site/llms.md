# Lexab

> Lexab ({{ORIGIN}}) is an AI platform for contract review. It finds risks in a contract, proposes edits and answers questions about the document. Every reference to a legal provision is checked in code against a corpus of official legislation. Main market: Uzbekistan (lawyers and businesses working in Russian and Uzbek); six more jurisdictions are covered.

Key facts (checked against the production database on 23 November 2026):

- Jurisdictions: Uzbekistan, United Kingdom, Kazakhstan, Germany, USA (federal level), Canada (Quebec), UAE.
- Corpus: 192 official documents, 21,868 provisions. Uzbekistan: 175 documents and 12,751 provisions — the Constitution, codes and laws, 26 decrees and resolutions of the President, and 66 resolutions of the Plenum of the Supreme Court.
- Sources: official government portals only — lex.uz, legislation.gov.uk, adilet.zan.kz, gesetze-im-internet.de, govinfo.gov, legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca, uaelegislation.gov.ae. Every stored provision carries its source URL, checksum and retrieval date. Statutory text is never written by the AI model.
- Citation check: a finding whose citation cannot be confirmed in the corpus is marked "unverified".
- Interface languages: Russian, Uzbek, English, Kazakh, German, Arabic.
- Plans: Free ($0, no card needed), Standard ($15/month), Pro ($50/month), Business ($499/month), Enterprise on request.

What Lexab does not do:

- It does not replace a lawyer: a review and a citation are grounds for a decision, not the decision.
- The corpus has no court decisions on individual cases. For Uzbekistan it includes resolutions of the Plenum of the Supreme Court; for the other jurisdictions there is no case law.
- It stores the version of each law currently in force; there are no point-in-time versions.
- There is no e-signature: documents are signed the usual way.
